
Paragraph 6 theme: Contributions of this paper.
This paper makes several contributions to the field of blink detection in fatigue-driving research. First, we formalise the problem of blink detection in the epoch-structured setting and demonstrate the limitations of naive thresholding approaches that do not account for the epoch structure. Second, we propose a novel three-stage pipeline for blink detection that includes epoch screening, sample-level threshold estimation, and blink-region extraction, which explicitly handles the challenges of the epoch-structured setting and pre-rejected epochs. Third, we introduce a robust threshold estimator based on the median and median absolute deviation (MAD) and systematically compare it against mean-based and directly optimised alternatives. Finally, we provide a comprehensive benchmark of competing methods across different epoch durations, sensitivity analyses for key parameters, and a morphological analysis of detection failures, all evaluated on a large naturalistic driving EEG corpus under an event-level overlap criterion with macro-averaged F1 and Wilcoxon signed-rank tests.
