    \caption{Epoch-aware double-thresholding pipeline for multichannel EEG blink-region detection. Continuous EEG signals from FP1, AF3, F3, FP2, AF4, and F4 are downsampled to 100 Hz, band-pass filtered from 1–20 Hz, and segmented into non-overlapping 30-s epochs after low-health epochs are removed. Stage A applies channel-specific peak-to-peak screening and a multichannel union rule to select suspicious epochs; E2, E5, and E7 form the candidate set in this example. Stage B pools and concatenates samples from these candidate epochs to estimate a robust sample-level threshold separately for each channel. Stage C applies the resulting channel-specific threshold to all valid epochs, including candidate and non-candidate epochs, and reports each detected blink region by epoch ID, channel, onset time, and duration. Orange denotes Stage A candidate epochs, while red denotes detected blink regions.}
