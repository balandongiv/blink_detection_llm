\subsection{Problem Definition}\label{sec:eval}

Let a blink annotation be an interval \(g_i = [s_i, e_i]\) and a detector output be an interval \(d_j = [\hat{s}_j, \hat{e}_j]\). A detected region is counted as a true positive if it overlaps at least one unmatched ground-truth region. Unmatched detections are false positives; unmatched ground-truth regions are false negatives. This event-level treatment follows the logic of interval-based detection rather than pointwise accuracy, which is appropriate because blink boundaries are uncertain, annotation granularity varies, and non-event samples dominate the timeline \citep{saito2015precision,salles2024softed}.
