
\paragraph{Paired comparisons.}
The recording session was the unit of analysis. Differences between detector
conditions were assessed using the Wilcoxon signed-rank test applied to matched
pairs of session-level $F_1$ scores. This non-parametric paired test was selected
because $F_1$ is bounded and no normality assumption was imposed on the paired
differences. All inferential tests reported in the main analyses were
two-sided, and zero-valued paired differences were omitted according to the
Wilcoxon convention.

\paragraph{Multiplicity and effect size.}
The family-wise significance level was set to $\alpha=0.05$. Bonferroni
adjustment was applied separately within each prespecified family of related
comparisons. The four-condition strategy analysis comprised six pairwise tests,
whereas the epoch-duration analysis comprised six comparisons between the
non-reference durations and the 30\,s reference. Channel-subset comparisons were
corrected separately within each corpus over the 13 subsets tested against the
full 32-electrode montage. Analysis-specific family sizes for any additional
comparisons are stated with the corresponding results. Bonferroni-adjusted
$p$-values are reported together with the matched-pairs rank-biserial
correlation $r$ as an effect-size measure; the sign of $r$ indicates the
direction of the paired difference in the stated comparison.
