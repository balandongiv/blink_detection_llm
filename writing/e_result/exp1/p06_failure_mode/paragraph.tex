In contrast to the frontal subsets, the non-frontal subsets showed markedly lower $F_1$ than the full montage in both Internal and Cao2018 (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_subset_summary}). On Cao2018, the parietal, occipital, and posterior subsets reached $F_1$ values of 16.68\%, 11.97\%, and 17.36\%, respectively, whereas the same subsets reached only 2.64\%, 4.06\%, and 4.26\% on Internal.
