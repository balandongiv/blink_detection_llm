In contrast to the frontal subsets, the non-frontal subsets showed markedly lower region-mean $F_1$ than their own electrodes scored inside the full montage, in both Internal and Cao2018 (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_subset_summary}). On Cao2018, the parietal, occipital, and posterior subsets reached $F_1$ values of 15.00\%, 6.42\%, and 8.62\%, respectively, whereas the same subsets reached only 1.72\%, 2.16\%, and 2.08\% on Internal.
