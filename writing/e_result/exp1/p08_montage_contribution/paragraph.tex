In contrast to the subset analyses, which tested whether channels were sufficient on their own, this comparison measured what the rest of the montage contributed by scoring each electrode alone and again within the full 32-channel Proposed-Med run on the same sessions, where Stage A screened epochs using every electrode (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_solo_vs_montage}). At Fp1 and Fp2, the other electrodes produced no significant $F_1$ gain on either corpus: in Raja, the full-montage differences were $+0.0259$ for Fp1 ($p=1.000$) and $+0.0345$ for Fp2 ($p=1.000$), while in Cao2018 they were $+0.0203$ for Fp1 ($p=1.000$) and $+0.0286$ for Fp2 ($p=0.991$). At every weaker electrode, the gain was significant: in Raja, the differences were $+0.0516$ for AF4 ($p=0.001$), $+0.0617$ for AF3 ($p=0.002$), $+0.0579$ for F3 ($p=0.008$), and $+0.0526$ for F4 ($p<0.001$), and in Cao2018 they were $+0.1021$ for F3 ($p<0.001$) and $+0.0896$ for F4 ($p<0.001$). For these weaker electrodes, the gain appeared in recall rather than precision.
