Consistent with the channel-subset results, the complete Proposed-Med pipeline retained most of the full-montage performance when operated on a single frontopolar electrode, whereas performance dropped sharply for single electrodes outside the frontopolar pair. The best single-electrode $F_1$ reached 0.8373 for Fp1 on Raja, corresponding to 94.9 percent of the full-montage reference, and 0.7765 for Fp1 on Cao2018, corresponding to 96.3 percent. These single-electrode values involved no channel selection because only one electrode was available, so, unlike every other condition in the article, they carried no best-channel-per-session oracle and therefore represented the values that a deployed single-electrode system would produce. Outside the frontopolar pair, on Raja, $F_1$ was 0.7516 for AF4, 0.6759 for AF3, 0.3985 for F3, and 0.3998 for F4, while on Cao2018 it was 0.4930 for F3 and 0.4903 for F4 (Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_single_channel}).
