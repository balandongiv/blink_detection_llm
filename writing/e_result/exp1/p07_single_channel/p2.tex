On Internal (Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_single_channel_stats}A), every electrode scored lower in the single-electrode run than in the full-montage run, with $\Delta F_1$ ranging from $-7.02$ percentage points at C3 to $-0.15$ percentage points at CP2. These reductions were significant after correction for 14 of the 24 electrodes (adjusted $p < 0.05$). Fp1 and Fp2 retained the two highest single-electrode scores, and neither differed significantly from the full-montage run (adjusted $p > 0.05$ for both). In contrast, the other four frontal electrodes, AF4, AF3, F4 and F3, were all significantly lower. In the central region, the reduction was significant for C3, FC5 and C4 but not for FC6, FC2 or FC1. In the parietal region, only CP1 was significantly lower, whereas CP2, CP5 and CP6 were not. In the occipital region, as defined in Figure~\ref{fig:eeg_map}, the reduction was significant for PO3, PO4, E83, P3, P8 and P7, leaving O1 and P4 as the only exceptions. Table~\ref{tab:app_single_internal} in Appendix~\ref{app:per_electrode_stats} lists the session-mean $F_1$, $\Delta F_1$ and adjusted $p$-value for every electrode.
