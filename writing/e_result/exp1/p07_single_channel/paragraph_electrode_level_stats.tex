The descriptive differences between single-electrode and full-montage operation were next tested for every electrode using session-paired comparisons. For each electrode, its session-level $F_1$ when used as the only input channel was paired with its session-level $F_1$ in the full-montage run, using the sessions common to both. For example, the $F_1$ of Fp2 when the pipeline was run on Fp2 alone was compared with the $F_1$ of Fp2 when all 32 channels were supplied to the pipeline, and the same comparison was made for every other electrode.
%
Each electrode was tested with a two-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank test, paired by session, with $p$ Bonferroni-corrected over the electrodes tested within each dataset
The Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_single_channel_stats} shows, for every electrode, its session-mean $F_1$ operating alone overlaid on the same electrode's session-mean $F_1$ under the full montage, together with the adjusted-significance marker.

On Internal (Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_single_channel_stats}A), 14 of the 24 electrodes remained significantly different after correction. Every electrode scored lower in the single-electrode run, with $\Delta F_1$ ranging from $-7.02$ to $-0.15$ percentage points. Although Fp1 and Fp2 retained the two highest single-electrode scores, neither differed significantly from the full-montage run after correction (adjusted $p = 1.000$ for both). In contrast, AF4 and AF3 were significantly lower in the single-electrode run than in the full-montage run. Beyond the frontal region, both C3 and C4 showed a significant configuration effect despite the large C3--C4 asymmetry noted above. Most occipital electrodes also differed significantly, with the exception of O1 and P4, even though all of them had very low absolute $F_1$.


On Cao2018 (Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_single_channel_stats}B), 16 of the 18 electrodes remained significantly different after correction, and all differences were again reductions. As on Internal, only the frontopolar pair Fp1 and Fp2 did not reach significance after correction (adjusted $p = 1.000$ for both). F3 and F4, which had the third- and fourth-highest single-electrode scores, showed the two largest reductions ($\Delta F_1 = -10.21$ and $-8.96$ percentage points, respectively). Every central, parietal, and occipital electrode differed significantly, including both C3 and C4.
