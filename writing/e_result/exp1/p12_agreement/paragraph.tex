Despite the small cost of using a fixed frontopolar choice, Experiment 2 showed that agreement on the best electrode across the four detection conditions was limited, with low full agreement on the exact electrode in both corpora and in the pooled analysis (Table~\ref{tab:channel-robustness}). All four conditions selected the same electrode in 9 of 46 Internal sessions (0.20), 6 of 58 Cao2018 sessions (0.10), and 15 of 104 pooled sessions (0.14). Mean pairwise agreement was 0.49 for Internal, 0.41 for Cao2018, and 0.44 when pooled. Agreement was highest for Proposed-Med, with values of 0.60 for Internal, 0.51 for Cao2018, and 0.55 pooled, whereas MNE-annot had the lowest agreement, with corresponding values of 0.33, 0.22, and 0.27.
