Despite the small cost of using a fixed frontopolar choice, Experiment 2 showed that agreement on the best electrode across the four detection conditions was limited, with low full agreement on the exact electrode in both corpora and in the pooled analysis (Table~\ref{tab:channel-robustness}). All four conditions selected the same electrode in 9 of 46 Raja sessions (0.196), 6 of 58 Cao2018 sessions (0.103), and 15 of 104 pooled sessions (0.144). Mean pairwise agreement was 0.489 for Raja, 0.408 for Cao2018, and 0.444 when pooled. Agreement was highest for Proposed-Med, with values of 0.601 for Raja, 0.511 for Cao2018, and 0.551 pooled, whereas MNE-annot had the lowest agreement, with corresponding values of 0.326, 0.218, and 0.266.
