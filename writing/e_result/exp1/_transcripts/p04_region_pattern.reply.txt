Consistent with the preceding per-electrode pattern, the region-collapsed results showed a strong anterior-to-posterior decline in Proposed-Med performance, although the decline was not strictly monotonic on Internal because occipital $F_1$ slightly exceeded parietal $F_1$ (Figure~\ref{fig:region_performance}). On Internal, frontal electrodes ($n=4$) yielded 75.64% precision, 56.76% recall, and 61.24% $F_1$, compared with 36.26%, 12.88%, and 14.80%, respectively, for central electrodes ($n=6$), 14.09%, 1.10%, and 1.94% for parietal electrodes ($n=4$), and 12.47%, 1.38%, and 2.30% for occipital electrodes ($n=8$). On Cao2018, the decrease was monotonic from frontal ($n=2$; 72.17% precision, 54.05% recall, 58.75% $F_1$) to central ($n=6$; 62.57%, 31.23%, 38.13%), parietal ($n=2$; 48.70%, 13.32%, 18.70%), and occipital electrodes ($n=6$; 34.04%, 5.29%, 8.24%), with both precision and recall decreasing across successive regions. When frontal and frontopolar electrodes were combined, their mean $F_1$ was 69.68% across $n=6$ electrodes on Internal, compared with 8.05% across the $n=26$ other electrodes, while on Cao2018 the corresponding means were 68.91% across $n=4$ frontal and frontopolar electrodes and 23.95% across the $n=28$ other electrodes.
