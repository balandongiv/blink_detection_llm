Across the channel-selection analyses, best-electrode selection concentrated on the frontopolar pair Fp1 and Fp2, although which member was selected most often varied by corpus and condition (Table~\ref{tab:channel_selection}; Figure~\ref{fig:channel_selection}). In Experiment~1, using the full montage, Raja selected Fp1 in 21 of 46 sessions (0.457) and Fp2 in 19 of 46 sessions (0.413), whereas Cao2018 selected Fp1 in 34 of 58 sessions (0.586) and Fp2 in 15 of 58 sessions (0.259). When selections were pooled over the four detection conditions of Experiment~2, Raja selected Fp2 most frequently, with 75 of 184 selections (0.408), followed by Fp1 with 67 of 184 (0.364), whereas Cao2018 selected Fp1 most frequently, with 119 of 232 selections (0.513), followed by Fp2 with 57 of 232 (0.246). Thus, although selection remained concentrated on Fp1 and Fp2, the pooled Experiment~2 results favoured Fp2 in Raja and Fp1 in Cao2018.
