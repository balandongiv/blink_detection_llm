Against the channel-subset conditions, the full 32-electrode montage served as the reference against which every subset was measured because it assumed no prior knowledge of where blinks projected. On Raja, the full montage achieved a precision of 0.8803, recall of 0.9014, and $F_1$ of 0.8825, whereas on Cao2018 it achieved a precision of 0.7591, recall of 0.8909, and $F_1$ of 0.8068. This condition was identical to the Proposed-Med condition of Experiment~2 (Section~\ref{sec:exp2_strategy}) and the 30-second condition of Experiment~3 (Section~\ref{sec:exp3_epoch}) on every session in both corpora: 46 of 46 Raja sessions and 58 of 58 Cao2018 sessions produced identical per-session values, with a maximum absolute $F_1$ difference of 0.0000 in each corpus. The same full-montage configuration was carried unchanged into Experiments~2 and~3.
