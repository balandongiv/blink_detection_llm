Following the preceding analysis, Experiment 1 evaluated whether channel subsets were sufficient on their own by running the complete Stage A--Stage C Proposed-Med pipeline separately on each subset in both Raja and Cao2018, using 30-second epochs and the median centre. Raja comprised 46 sessions and 20 channel subsets, including 14 anatomical subsets with the full montage and 6 single-electrode subsets, whereas Cao2018 comprised 58 sessions and 18 channel subsets, including 14 anatomical subsets with the full montage and 4 single-electrode subsets; in each corpus, 13 subsets were compared against the full montage. Performance was aggregated by selecting the best channel for each session and then averaging over sessions. Comparisons with the full montage used two-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction within each dataset, and matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation was reported as the effect size. Because Stages A--C were rerun using each subset alone, each subset constituted a self-contained detector, so this design measured whether a subset was sufficient on its own; it was not a leave-one-region-out analysis and did not establish that any region was necessary to the complete system. Raja electrodes were reported by their 10--20 scalp locations, with the corresponding native EGI indices given in Table~\ref{tab:egi_map}.
