Consistent with the regional differences above, the number of electrodes given to Proposed-Med predicted performance poorly, whereas their scalp location predicted it well (Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_coverage_curve}). On Raja, Fp1 alone achieved an $F_1$ of 0.8373, compared with 0.5859 for the six-electrode Central subset and 0.0426 for the 12-electrode Posterior subset; on Cao2018, Fp1 alone achieved 0.7765, compared with 0.4668 for the six-electrode Central subset and 0.1736 for the eight-electrode Posterior subset. Variation also remained large at a fixed subset size: $F_1$ among the single-electrode subsets spanned 0.3985 to 0.8373 on Raja and 0.1480 to 0.7765 on Cao2018. Each subset re-ran Stage A, Stage B, and Stage C using that subset alone, so these comparisons evaluated whether a subset was sufficient for detection rather than measuring the necessity of a region to the complete system.
