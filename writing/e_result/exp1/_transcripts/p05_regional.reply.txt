Consistent with the preceding per-electrode regional results, the anatomical subset reruns preserved the strong frontal advantage but yielded significantly lower region-mean $F_1$ than the same regions' electrodes scored inside the full montage on both Raja and Cao2018 (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_subset_summary}; Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_region_boxplot}). Each subset was a self-contained Proposed-Med detector in which Stages A, B and C were rerun using only that subset, and the paired comparison was therefore against the same region's electrodes in the full-montage run rather than against the full-montage headline oracle $F_1$. After Bonferroni correction, every anatomical subset differed significantly from its corresponding full-montage electrodes in both corpora, with no non-significant subsets in either Raja or Cao2018. For the frontal subset, Raja gave $F_1$ subset $=0.6692$ and $F_1$ montage $=0.6968$, with $\Delta F_1=-0.0276$ and $p<0.001$, whereas Cao2018 gave $F_1$ subset $=0.6661$ and $F_1$ montage $=0.6891$, with $\Delta F_1=-0.0229$ and $p=0.026$; despite statistical significance, these were modest absolute changes in $F_1$, although the Raja frontal decrease was larger than those of the other reported coarse regions, while the Cao2018 decrease was smaller than the central and parietal decreases and similar to the posterior decrease. The corresponding Raja values for central, parietal, occipital and posterior subsets were respectively $0.1399/0.1480/-0.0081$, $0.0172/0.0194/-0.0022$, $0.0216/0.0230/-0.0015$ and $0.0208/0.0218/-0.0011$ for $F_1$ subset, $F_1$ montage and $\Delta F_1$, while Cao2018 gave $0.3429/0.3813/-0.0383$, $0.1500/0.1870/-0.0369$, $0.0642/0.0824/-0.0183$ and $0.0862/0.1086/-0.0224$, respectively. Thus, consistent with the per-electrode region pattern, frontal electrodes still vastly outperformed the central, parietal, occipital and posterior regions in absolute $F_1$ in both the subset-only reruns and the full-montage run. All matched-pairs rank-biserial effect sizes were large, and every difference had the same direction, with subset $F_1$ below montage $F_1$.
