Under single-electrode operation, Proposed-Med retained near-reference $F_1$ only at the frontopolar pair, whereas performance fell sharply at the other electrodes (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_solo_vs_montage}; Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_single_channel}). On Raja, the best single-electrode result was obtained at Fp1 with $F_1=0.8373$, corresponding to 94.9 percent of the full-montage reference, while Fp2 reached $F_1=0.8337$ and AF4 and AF3 fell to $F_1=0.7516$ and $F_1=0.6759$, respectively; the weakest electrode was F3 at $F_1=0.3985$, corresponding to 45.2 percent of the full-montage reference. On Cao2018, Fp1 was also best with $F_1=0.7765$, corresponding to 96.3 percent of the full-montage reference, whereas F3 and F4 reached $F_1=0.4930$ and $F_1=0.4903$, respectively, with F4 the weakest at 60.8 percent of the full-montage reference. After Bonferroni correction, scoring the same electrode inside the full montage rather than alone did not significantly increase $F_1$ at Raja Fp1 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0259$, $p=1.000$) or Fp2 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0345$, $p=1.000$), or at Cao2018 Fp1 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0203$, $p=1.000$) or Fp2 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0286$, $p=0.991$). In contrast, the increase was significant at every weaker electrode: Raja AF4 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0516$, $p=0.001$), AF3 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0617$, $p=0.002$), F3 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0579$, $p=0.008$), and F4 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0526$, $p<0.001$), and Cao2018 F3 ($\Delta F_1=+0.1021$, $p<0.001$) and F4 ($\Delta F_1=+0.0896$, $p<0.001$). For these weaker electrodes, the gain was concentrated in recall rather than precision: for Raja AF4, recall increased from 0.7010 alone to 0.7767 in the full montage while precision changed from 0.8765 to 0.8691, and for Cao2018 F3, recall increased from 0.4323 to 0.5495 while precision changed from 0.7132 to 0.7264.
Consistent with the electrode-level comparison, collapsing the single-electrode selections to their region mean provided a second, coarser comparison, and because every evaluated single electrode in both corpora belonged to the frontal region, this yielded one frontal-region comparison per corpus rather than four regions (Table~\ref{tab:region_performance}; Table~\ref{tab:exp1_subset_summary}). On Raja, the single-electrode-alone region mean was $F_1=0.6495$, compared with $F_1=0.6968$ for the same electrodes scored within the full-montage run, with $\Delta F_1=-0.0474$; a single session-paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test gave $p<0.001$ and an effect size of $r=-0.798$. On Cao2018, the corresponding values were $F_1=0.6289$ for the single-electrode-alone region mean and $F_1=0.6891$ within the full-montage run, with $\Delta F_1=-0.0602$, $p<0.001$, and $r=-0.716$. Each corpus therefore required only one Wilcoxon signed-rank comparison at this coarse level, so no Bonferroni factor was applied. For comparison, running the same frontal electrodes together as one self-contained multi-electrode subset produced $F_1=0.6692$ on Raja and $F_1=0.6661$ on Cao2018, higher than the corresponding single-electrode-alone region means but below the same electrodes' full-montage region means of $F_1=0.6968$ and $F_1=0.6891$, respectively.
