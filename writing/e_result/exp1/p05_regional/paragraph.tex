Following the preceding analysis, Experiment 1 tested whether channel subsets were sufficient as self-contained detectors by running the complete Stage A--Stage C Proposed-Med pipeline separately on each subset in both Internal and Cao2018, using 30-second epochs and the median centre. Internal comprised 46 sessions and 20 channel subsets, including 14 anatomical subsets with the full montage and 6 single-electrode subsets, whereas Cao2018 comprised 58 sessions and 18 channel subsets, including 14 anatomical subsets with the full montage and 4 single-electrode subsets; in each corpus, 13 subsets were compared against the full montage.

Performance was aggregated by selecting the best channel for each session and then averaging over sessions. Comparisons with the full montage used two-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni correction within each dataset, and matched-pairs rank-biserial correlation was reported as the effect size. Because Stages A--C were rerun using each subset alone, each subset constituted a self-contained detector, so this design measured whether a subset was sufficient on its own; it was not a leave-one-region-out analysis and did not establish that any region was necessary to the complete system. Internal electrodes were reported by their 10--20 scalp locations.

At the anatomical-subset level, Proposed-Med retained performance comparable to the full montage only for frontal configurations, whereas central and more posterior subsets performed substantially worse (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_subset_summary}; Figure~\ref{fig:exp1_region_boxplot}). The Frontal subset was statistically indistinguishable from the full montage on both corpora, with $F_1=86.76\%$, a paired difference of $-1.49$ percentage points, and $p=1.000$ on Internal, and $F_1=79.61\%$, a paired difference of $-1.06$ percentage points, and $p=1.000$ on Cao2018.
% Self Note: a $p$-value at or near unity indicates that the observed difference is fully consistent with no true difference from the full montage, i.e.\ the strongest possible evidence of equivalence rather than of a significant effect.
After Bonferroni correction, Frontal, Frontal-Left and Frontal-Right did not differ significantly from the full montage on Internal, with $p=1.000$, $p=0.070$ and $p=0.113$, respectively. On Cao2018, only Frontal and Frontal-Left remained non-significant, with $p=1.000$ and $p=0.123$, whereas Frontal-Right differed significantly from the full montage, with $p=0.001$.

As expected, performance fell steeply when only central, parietal, occipital or posterior subsets were used. Internal yielded $F_1=58.59\%$ for Central, $F_1=2.64\%$ for Parietal, $F_1=4.06\%$ for Occipital and $F_1=4.26\%$ for Posterior, while Cao2018 yielded $F_1=46.68\%$, $F_1=16.68\%$, $F_1=11.97\%$ and $F_1=17.36\%$ for the corresponding subsets. All differences for these central, parietal, occipital and posterior subsets remained significant after correction, with large matched-pairs rank-biserial effect sizes ($p<0.001$ in every case; for Central, $r=-1.00$ on Internal and $r=-0.99$ on Cao2018).
