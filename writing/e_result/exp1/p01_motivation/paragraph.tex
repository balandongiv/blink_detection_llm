In the proposed pipeline, the Stage A screened epochs using a union rule over the channels provided to the pipeline, so changing the channel set, in theory, may also change which epochs reached Stage B.
Restricting the available channels into pipeline, therefore changed the detector itself rather than merely changing the signals presented to a fixed detector.
Therefore, the channel-subset analysis was performed by re-running the complete pipeline with each channel subset rather than evaluating a detector trained on a different channel set. But as a baseline, we first show how the detector performed when trained and tested on the full channel set, on a subset of the channels, and on a single channel.
