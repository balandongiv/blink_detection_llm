Against this full-montage reference, per-electrode detection quality was concentrated at the frontopolar pair rather than distributed across the frontal region, with each electrode scored within the 32-channel Proposed-Med run in which Stage A screened epochs using every electrode (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_channel_ablation}, Table~\ref{tab:region_performance}, and Figure~\ref{fig:region_performance}). On Raja, Fp2 and Fp1 reached $F_1$ values of 0.8682 and 0.8632, respectively, before performance fell at the neighbouring frontal sites AF4 and AF3 to 0.8032 and 0.7376, with Fz, F3, and F4 at 0.6416, 0.4565, and 0.4523; the lowest value was 0.0175 at CP6.

On Cao2018, Fp1 and Fp2 reached $F_1$ values of 0.7968 and 0.7845, respectively, whereas the neighbouring frontal sites F7, F8, Fz, F3, and F4 were lower at 0.6028, 0.5971, 0.5970, 0.5951, and 0.5799; the lowest value was 0.0281 at Oz.

The central electrodes also showed a dataset-specific asymmetry: on Raja, C3 reached $F_1=0.6106$ while C4 reached $F_1=0.0185$, whereas on Cao2018 C3 and C4 were close at $F_1=0.3108$ and $F_1=0.3018$, respectively, so the pronounced C3--C4 asymmetry was specific to Raja.
