Against this full-montage reference, per-electrode detection quality was concentrated at the frontopolar pair rather than distributed across the frontal region, with each electrode scored within the 32-channel Proposed-Med run in which Stage A screened epochs using every electrode (Figure~\ref{fig:region_performance}). On Internal, Fp2 and Fp1 reached $F_1$ values of 86.82\% and 86.32\%, respectively, before performance fell at the neighbouring frontal sites AF4 and AF3 to 80.32\% and 73.76\%, with F3, and F4 at 45.65\%, and 45.23\%; the lowest value was 1.75\% at CP6.

On Cao2018, Fp1 and Fp2 reached $F_1$ values of 79.68\% and 78.45\%, respectively, whereas the neighbouring frontal sites F3 and F4 were lower at 59.51\% and 57.99\%; the lowest value was 3.10\% at O1.

The central electrodes also showed a dataset-specific asymmetry. On Internal, C3 reached $F_1=61.06\%$ while C4 reached $F_1=1.85\%$, whereas on Cao2018 C3 and C4 were close at $F_1=31.08\%$ and $F_1=30.18\%$, respectively, so the pronounced C3--C4 asymmetry was specific to Internal.

Consistent with the preceding per-electrode pattern, the region-collapsed results showed a strong anterior-to-posterior decline in Proposed-Med performance, although the decline was not strictly monotonic on Internal because occipital $F_1$ slightly exceeded parietal $F_1$ (Table~\ref{tab:region_performance} and Figure~\ref{fig:region_performance}). On Internal, frontal electrodes ($n=6$, yielded 79.21\% precision, 67.62\% recall, and 69.68\% $F_1$, compared with 36.26\%, 12.88\%, and 14.80\%, respectively, for central electrodes ($n=6$), 14.09\%, 1.10\%, and 1.94\% for parietal electrodes ($n=4$), and 12.47\%, 1.38\%, and 2.30\% for occipital electrodes ($n=8$). On Cao2018, the decrease was monotonic from frontal 73.03\% precision, 71.64\% recall, 68.91\% $F_1$) to central ($n=6$; 62.57\%, 31.23\%, 38.13\%), parietal ($n=2$; 48.70\%, 13.32\%, 18.70\%), and occipital electrodes ($n=6$; 34.04\%, 5.29\%, 8.24\%), with both precision and recall decreasing across successive regions. The frontal mean $F_1$ of 69.68\% on Internal contrasted with 8.05\% across the $n=26$ other electrodes, while on Cao2018 the frontal mean of 68.91\% contrasted with 23.95\% across the $n=28$ other electrodes.
