Across all 104 Raja and Cao2018 sessions, the macro-averaged results in Table~\ref{tab:exp1_main} and Figure~\ref{fig:condition_prf} showed a clear separation between the two proposed configurations and the baselines. Proposed-Med achieved the highest macro-$F_1$, obtaining precision 0.8268, recall 0.8794, and $F_1$ 0.8392, followed closely by Proposed-Mean, which obtained precision 0.8356, recall 0.8604, and $F_1$ 0.8320. Both three-stage configurations clearly exceeded the BLINKER-concat and MNE-annot baselines in macro-$F_1$, with BLINKER-concat obtaining precision 0.6153, recall 0.9695, and $F_1$ 0.7242, and MNE-annot obtaining precision 0.6409, recall 0.6133, and $F_1$ 0.5731. By paired Wilcoxon tests with Bonferroni correction, Proposed-Med significantly outperformed both BLINKER-concat and MNE-annot, although its margin over Proposed-Mean was small and did not reach statistical significance. BLINKER-concat reached the highest recall across conditions, but this was accompanied by the lowest precision, indicating that its high sensitivity came with a substantially larger false-positive burden.
