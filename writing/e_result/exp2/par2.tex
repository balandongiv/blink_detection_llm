The pipeline was evaluated under epoch durations of 10, 20, 30 (reference), 40, 50, 60 and 120 seconds.
For each duration the full pipeline is re-run from scratch, since epoch-level
peak-to-peak statistics change with epoch length.
Table~\ref{tab:epoch_duration} reports results and two-tailed Wilcoxon $p$-values relative to the 30-second reference.
