The pipeline was evaluated under epoch durations of 10, 20, 30, 40, 50, 60, and 120~s, with 30~s serving as the reference duration. For each duration, the complete pipeline was rerun from scratch using the full 32-channel montage, since both the Stage~A peak-to-peak statistics and the samples pooled for the Stage~B thresholds change with epoch length. Keeping the full montage ensures that epoch duration is the only factor that differs from the reference. Experiment~1 found that Fp1 and Fp2 were consistently the two best-performing electrodes on both corpora. We therefore restrict the epoch-duration analysis to these two electrodes and report their $F_1$ from each full-montage run. The two electrodes are reported separately rather than averaged.




Figure~\ref{fig:f1_by_epoch} reports the session-mean $F_1$ of Fp1 and Fp2 for each duration. For each electrode, every non-reference duration was compared with the 30-s reference using a two-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank test paired by session, with $p$-values Bonferroni-corrected over the six non-reference durations. An asterisk marks durations that differ significantly from the reference (adjusted $p < 0.05$).
