Across the epoch-duration sweep, Proposed-Med showed stable pooled performance and stable performance on Cao2018, whereas Raja differed significantly from the 30-second reference at several durations (Table~\ref{tab:epoch_duration}, Figure~\ref{fig:f1_by_epoch}).
Pooled $F_1$ had a spread of 0.0039 across the seven durations, and no duration differed significantly from the 30-second reference after correction, with the smallest corrected p-value at 120~s ($p=0.067$).
Cao2018 likewise had no significant difference at any duration and showed a spread of 0.0046; the 120-second duration had the highest $F_1$ of 0.8112 but did not differ significantly from the 30-second reference ($p=0.659$).
Raja was not uniformly stable: relative to the 30-second reference, $F_1$ was 0.8793 at 50~s ($p<0.001$), 0.8773 at 60~s ($p=0.002$), and 0.8688 at 120~s ($p<0.001$), with all three differences significant after correction.
The 30-second reference had the highest Raja $F_1$ of 0.8825 among the seven durations.
