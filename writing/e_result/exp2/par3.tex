Performance on Cao2018 was also stable for both electrodes: the $F_1$ spread across the seven durations was 0.38 percentage points for Fp1 and 0.33 percentage points for Fp2, with no significant difference at any tested duration for either. The 30~s reference produced the lowest Cao2018 $F_1$ of the seven durations for both electrodes, 79.68\% for Fp1 and 78.45\% for Fp2; the highest values, Fp1's 80.05\% at 60~s and Fp2's 78.78\% at 120~s, exceeded the reference by no more than 0.38 percentage points and did not differ significantly from it, with $p=0.882$ for Fp1 and $p=1.000$ for Fp2.

Internal showed a less consistent, and electrode-dependent, pattern. The 30~s reference yielded each electrode's highest Internal $F_1$: 86.32\% for Fp1 and 86.82\% for Fp2. Relative to it, Fp1's $F_1$ fell significantly to 85.92\% at 50~s ($p=0.002$), to 85.67\% at 60~s ($p<0.001$) and to 85.04\% at 120~s ($p<0.001$), whereas Fp2 only differed significantly at 120~s, falling to 85.57\% ($p<0.001$): Fp2's 50~s and 60~s declines, to 86.51\% and 86.27\% respectively, were descriptively similar to Fp1's but did not reach significance after correction, with $p=0.080$ and $p=0.064$ respectively. Neither electrode differed from its reference at 10, 20 or 40~s.
Significance tracked the consistency of the change rather than its magnitude. For Fp1, the 0.40-percentage-point mean drop at 50~s was significant because it recurred across sessions, 35 of 46 falling below the reference with a median change of $-0.44$ percentage points, whereas the larger 0.61-percentage-point mean drop at 10~s was not ($p=1.000$): sessions split 22 below against 24 above the reference and the median session improved by 0.13 percentage points, so the mean was carried by a minority of sessions with large declines. The same pattern held for Fp2.
The evidence therefore supports stable Cao2018 performance over the full 10--120~s range for both Fp1 and Fp2, and stable Internal performance from 10 to 40~s for both electrodes, but it does not support equivalence on Internal at 50~s and beyond for Fp1, or at 120~s for Fp2.
