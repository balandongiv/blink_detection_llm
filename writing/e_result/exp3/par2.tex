Within the two proposed configurations, the Stage-B threshold estimators produced similar overall performance, although the direction of the metric changes did not support the stated precision hypothesis (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_main}). On Raja, Proposed-Med achieved precision, recall, and $F_1$ of 0.8803, 0.9014, and 0.8825, compared with 0.8840, 0.8876, and 0.8742 for Proposed-Mean. The corresponding changes were -0.0038 in precision, +0.0138 in recall, and +0.0083 in $F_1$. On Cao2018, Proposed-Med likewise reduced precision by -0.0116 while increasing recall by +0.0190 and $F_1$ by +0.0037. Pooled performance followed the same pattern, with precision decreasing from 0.8209 to 0.8127, recall increasing from 0.8789 to 0.8956, and $F_1$ increasing from 0.8345 to 0.8403. However, the two estimators were statistically indistinguishable under the two-sided Bonferroni-corrected Wilcoxon test ($p=1$). Proposed-Med exceeded Proposed-Mean in 46/104 sessions, was equal in 4, and was lower in 54, indicating a per-session outcome close to even.
