We therefore hypothesise that the pooled, continuous-signal strategy produces lower session-level $F_1$ than the epoch-aware pipeline because of a less favourable precision-recall trade-off. To test this hypothesis, \rev{the BLINKER-threshold baseline}, MNE, and the proposed pipeline were applied to every session of Internal and Cao2018 using 30-s epochs. All 32 channels were included to ensure a fair comparison with \rev{the BLINKER-threshold baseline}, which by default processes every channel provided to it. All three methods received the same epochs, band-pass filtered at 1--20~Hz and resampled to 100~Hz, and each baseline was run on the concatenation of these epochs. % REMOVED (issue I04/I22): BLINKER was represented by its threshold-crossing stage but blink marphological-fitting step and automatic channel selection were not used.
