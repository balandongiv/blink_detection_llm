We therefore hypothesise that the pooled, continuous-signal strategy produces lower session-level $F_1$ than the epoch-aware pipeline because of a less favourable precision-recall trade-off. To test this hypothesis, the BLINKER-threshold baseline, MNE, and the proposed pipeline were applied to every session of Internal and Cao2018 using 30-s epochs. All 32 channels were included to ensure a fair comparison with the BLINKER-threshold baseline, which by default processes every channel provided to it. All three methods received the same epochs, band-pass filtered at 1--20~Hz and resampled to 100~Hz, and each baseline was run on the concatenation of these epochs.
The BLINKER-threshold baseline was represented by the initial threshold-crossing stage of the BLINKER algorithm, its blink morphological fitting step and automatic channel selection were not used. This baseline therefore evaluates the threshold-estimation component rather than the complete published BLINKER pipeline. MNE was represented by \texttt{mne.preprocessing.find\_eog\_events}. Both baselines were run on each channel independently.
For each method and session, the channel with the highest $F_1$ was then retained.
