Within the two proposed configurations, the Stage-B threshold estimators produced similar overall performance, although the direction of the metric changes did not support the stated precision hypothesis (Table~\ref{tab:exp3_estimator}).
On Internal, Proposed-Med achieved precision, recall, and $F_1$ of 88.03\%, 90.14\%, and 88.25\%, compared with 88.40\%, 88.76\%, and 87.42\% for Proposed-Mean. The corresponding changes were $-0.37$ percentage points in precision, $+1.38$ in recall, and $+0.83$ in $F_1$. On Cao2018, Proposed-Med likewise reduced precision by $-1.16$ percentage points while increasing recall by $+1.89$ and $F_1$ by $+0.38$. Pooled performance followed the same pattern, with precision decreasing from 82.09\% to 81.27\%, recall increasing from 87.89\% to 89.56\%, and $F_1$ increasing from 83.45\% to 84.03\%. However, the two estimators were statistically indistinguishable under the two-sided Bonferroni-corrected Wilcoxon test ($p=1$). Proposed-Med exceeded Proposed-Mean in 46/104 sessions, was equal in 4, and was lower in 54, indicating a per-session outcome close to even.
