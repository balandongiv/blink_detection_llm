
Stage~B estimates the sample-level threshold from samples pooled across the suspicious
epoch set $\mathcal{S}$.
Because $\mathcal{S}$ is selected precisely because it contains
high-amplitude excursions, the pooled distribution is not symmetric around a clean baseline such that it is contaminated by the very peaks the threshold is meant to capture.
The mean and standard deviation are sensitive to these extreme values wheraas the median and MAD are not.
The hypothesis is that the proposed-Med will outperform Proposed-Mean on precision---because the MAD-based threshold is less inflated by within-epoch peaks---while achieving comparable or superior recall.
