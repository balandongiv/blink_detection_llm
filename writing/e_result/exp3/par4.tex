The two baselines lost $F_1$ in different ways. On Internal (Figure~\ref{fig:exp3_pr_scatter}), \rev{the BLINKER-style baseline} favoured recall over precision, with a mean recall of 95.18\% and a mean precision of 67.94\%. On Cao2018 (Figure~\ref{fig:exp3_pr_scatter}), the same bias was stronger, with a mean recall of 97.15\% against a mean precision of 56.96\%. MNE, in contrast, showed no systematic bias, since its mean recall and precision differed by less than 2 percentage points on both datasets. Its operating points were instead widely dispersed, and recall fell below 30\% in several sessions on each dataset. The proposed pipeline had higher mean precision and recall than MNE on both datasets.
