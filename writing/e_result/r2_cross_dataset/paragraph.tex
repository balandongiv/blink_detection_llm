Across datasets, macro $F_1$ was consistently higher on Raja than on Cao2018 under every condition (Table~\ref{tab:cross_dataset_gap}; Figure~\ref{fig:f1_by_dataset}). BLINKER-concat achieved 0.7654 on Raja and 0.6940 on Cao2018, corresponding to a cross-dataset gap of +0.0714. MNE-annot achieved 0.6664 and 0.5627, respectively, with the largest gap of +0.1037. Proposed-Mean achieved 0.8742 on Raja and 0.8030 on Cao2018, producing the smallest gap of +0.0712, whereas Proposed-Med achieved 0.8825 and 0.8068, respectively, with a gap of +0.0758. The two proposed configurations and BLINKER-concat therefore maintained similar, narrow differences between the two corpora, while MNE-annot showed the least consistent performance across datasets.
