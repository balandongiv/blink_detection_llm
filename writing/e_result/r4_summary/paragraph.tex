Taken together, the three experiments showed that Proposed-Med consistently outperformed the best competing method, BLINKER-concat, on both Raja and Cao2018 (Table~\ref{tab:exp_summary}; Figure~\ref{fig:exp_boxplot}). At each experiment's primary configuration, pooled macro $F_1$ was 0.8493 for channel selection, 0.8403 for strategy comparison, and 0.8476 for the 30~s epoch duration, compared with 0.7256 for BLINKER-concat. The corresponding pooled mean advantages ranged from +0.1147 to +0.1237. The corpus-specific results followed the same ordering, with Proposed-Med achieving 0.8894, 0.8825, and 0.8882 on Raja and 0.8175, 0.8068, and 0.8153 on Cao2018 across Experiments 1--3, respectively. In the paired analyses, every bootstrap 95\% confidence interval excluded zero, and all six one-sided Wilcoxon comparisons remained significant after Bonferroni correction (Table~\ref{tab:exp_stats}). Thus, the advantage of Proposed-Med was consistent across experimental settings and corpora.
