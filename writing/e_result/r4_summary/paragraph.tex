Taken together, the three experiments showed that Proposed-Med consistently outperformed the best competing method, BLINKER-concat, on both Raja and Cao2018 (Table~\ref{tab:exp_summary}; Figure~\ref{fig:exp_boxplot}). The three experiments share a single primary configuration, the full montage at 30~s epochs with the median centre, and each reproduced it exactly: pooled macro $F_1$ was 0.8403 in all three, compared with 0.7256 for BLINKER-concat, a pooled advantage of +0.1147. The corpus-specific values were likewise identical across Experiments 1--3, at 0.8825 on Raja and 0.8068 on Cao2018. In the paired analyses, every bootstrap 95\% confidence interval excluded zero, and all six one-sided Wilcoxon comparisons remained significant after Bonferroni correction (Table~\ref{tab:exp_stats}). Thus, the advantage of Proposed-Med was consistent across experimental settings and corpora.
