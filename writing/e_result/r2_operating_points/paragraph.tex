Across the pooled and corpus-specific analyses, the precision--recall operating points showed a consistent ordering among the proposed and baseline conditions (Figure~\ref{fig:exp2_pr_scatter} and Figure~\ref{fig:pr_scatter}). In the pooled analysis, Proposed-Med and Proposed-Mean occupied the high-precision, high-recall region, with precision and recall of 81.27\% and 89.56\% for Proposed-Med and 82.09\% and 87.89\% for Proposed-Mean, respectively. BLINKER-concat occupied the high-recall, low-precision region, with recall of 96.27\% and precision of 61.82\%, whereas MNE-annot was lower on both measures, with precision of 64.25\% and recall of 65.64\%. The same ordering was observed within each corpus. On Internal, the proposed conditions had precision of 88.03\% and 88.40\% and recall of 88.76\% and 90.14\%, whereas BLINKER-concat combined 95.18\% recall with 67.94\% precision and MNE-annot achieved 69.44\% precision and 70.63\% recall. On Cao2018, the corresponding pattern remained, with the proposed conditions retaining higher precision and recall than MNE-annot, while BLINKER-concat retained the highest recall and lower precision.
