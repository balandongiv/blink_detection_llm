Across the pooled and corpus-specific analyses, the precision--recall operating points showed a consistent ordering among the proposed and baseline conditions (Figure~\ref{fig:exp2_pr_scatter} and Figure~\ref{fig:pr_scatter}). In the pooled analysis, Proposed-Med and Proposed-Mean occupied the high-precision, high-recall region, with precision and recall of 0.8127 and 0.8956 for Proposed-Med and 0.8209 and 0.8789 for Proposed-Mean, respectively. BLINKER-concat occupied the high-recall, low-precision region, with recall of 0.9627 and precision of 0.6182, whereas MNE-annot was lower on both measures, with precision of 0.6425 and recall of 0.6564. The same ordering was observed within each corpus. On Raja, the proposed conditions had precision of 0.8840 and 0.8803 and recall of 0.8876 and 0.9014, whereas BLINKER-concat combined 0.9518 recall with 0.6794 precision and MNE-annot achieved 0.6944 precision and 0.7063 recall. On Cao2018, the corresponding pattern remained, with the proposed conditions retaining higher precision and recall than MNE-annot, while BLINKER-concat retained the highest recall and lower precision.
