In the strategy comparison, the two proposed configurations achieved higher macro-$F_1$ than both baselines on Raja, Cao2018, and the pooled evaluation (Table~\ref{tab:exp1_main}). Proposed-Med obtained macro-$F_1$ values of 0.8825, 0.8068, and 0.8403, whereas Proposed-Mean obtained 0.8742, 0.8030, and 0.8345. The corresponding values were 0.7654, 0.6940, and 0.7256 for BLINKER-concat and 0.6664, 0.5627, and 0.6086 for MNE-annot. This separation resulted from the precision--recall profiles shown in Figure~\ref{fig:condition_prf}: BLINKER-concat produced the highest recall of any condition on Raja, Cao2018, and the pooled evaluation, at 0.9518, 0.9715, and 0.9627, but also the lowest precision, at 0.6794, 0.5696, and 0.6182. Wilcoxon signed-rank tests on session-level $F_1$, Bonferroni-corrected over the six pairwise comparisons, separated each proposed configuration from each baseline: Proposed-Med versus BLINKER-concat gave $p=1.32\times10^{-11}$ and versus MNE-annot $p=6.51\times10^{-9}$, while Proposed-Mean versus BLINKER-concat gave $p=4.97\times10^{-10}$ and versus MNE-annot $p=9.94\times10^{-9}$. Neither within-pair comparison was significant: BLINKER-concat versus MNE-annot yielded $p=0.812$, and Proposed-Mean versus Proposed-Med yielded $p=1$. No channel group on either dataset produced an inversion in which a baseline matched or exceeded Proposed-Med (Table~\ref{tab:exp2_inversions}).
