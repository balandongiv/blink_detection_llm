\paragraph{Error structure.}
The error-structure decomposition in Table~\ref{tab:error-structure} separates conditions by whether their mistakes are dominated by false positives or false negatives. BLINKER-concat is strongly FP-heavy, with 325.9 mean FP/session, 19.7 mean FN/session, and an FP:FN ratio of 16.512. MNE-annot is FN-heavy, with 65.6 mean FP/session, 430.0 mean FN/session, and an FP:FN ratio of 0.153. Proposed-Med is also FN-heavy, but its 49.8 mean FP/session and 213.8 mean FN/session give an FP:FN ratio of 0.233, which is higher than Proposed-Mean's 0.186 and below DBO's 1.533.
