The same regions and electrodes could be scored under three levels of channel context, each electrode operating independently, those electrodes operating together as the region's own self-contained subset, and the same electrodes scored inside the full montage, so a Friedman omnibus test across the three matched conditions was run for every region in both corpora, Bonferroni-corrected over the 5 regions tested within each dataset.
Every one of the 10 region-by-corpus omnibus tests reached significance after correction, the largest adjusted $p$-value being $0.001$ for Cao2018's frontal region and every other test below $0.0001$, so channel context reliably affected detection performance at every scalp region tested. Corrected pairwise Wilcoxon signed-rank contrasts (single-vs-subset, single-vs-full and subset-vs-full, each Bonferroni-corrected $\times 3$ within its region) showed a consistent monotonic improvement (single $<$ subset $<$ full montage) for every Internal region. Single-vs-subset was itself significant for Frontal ($\Delta F_1=-1.98$, adjusted $p=0.008$), Central ($-1.17$, adjusted $p<0.001$), Occipital ($-0.16$, adjusted $p<0.001$) and Posterior ($-0.17$, adjusted $p<0.001$), but not for Parietal, where the single-electrode and subset means were nearly identical ($1.71\%$ versus $1.72\%$, adjusted $p=0.745$). On Cao2018, Frontal and Central showed the same monotonic pattern (single-vs-subset adjusted $p<0.001$ and $p=0.019$, respectively), but Parietal, Occipital and Posterior did not. Their single-electrode means were marginally, non-significantly \emph{higher} than the corresponding regional-subset means ($15.51\%$ vs $15.00\%$, $6.60\%$ vs $6.42\%$, and $8.83\%$ vs $8.62\%$, all adjusted $p=1.000$), so at these low-performing posterior Cao2018 regions, pooling independent single-electrode detectors was statistically indistinguishable from running them together as one restricted-montage subset, even though both remained significantly below the full montage. Channel context therefore improved detection at every region in both corpora, but the specific step from isolated electrodes to a self-contained regional subset was not itself a reliable source of that improvement everywhere the data were tested.
