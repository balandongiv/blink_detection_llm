Using a long-closure threshold of 0.5~s to separate normal from long blinks, the ground truth comprised 105{,}763 normal blinks and 10{,}650 long blinks across Raja and Cao2018 (116{,}413 in total, with long blinks accounting for approximately 9.1\%). For Proposed-Med, event-level recall was consistently higher for normal than for long blinks: pooled recall was 0.8814 for normal blinks and 0.7868 for long blinks. The same ordering held within each corpus, with normal-versus-long recall of 0.8967 versus 0.7668 on Raja and 0.8693 versus 0.8027 on Cao2018, as shown in Table~\ref{tab:blink_type_recall}. Long blinks therefore remained a mild residual weakness for the threshold pipeline, with recall falling by approximately 0.09 from normal to long events.
