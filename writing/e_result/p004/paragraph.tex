Across 81,852 annotated normal blinks and 7,377 long blinks (89,229 total; long blinks accounting for approximately 8.3%), recall differed by blink duration across conditions: BLINKER-concat achieved 0.9848 for normal blinks and 0.9676 for long blinks, MNE-annot achieved 0.7761 and 0.7931, Proposed-Mean achieved 0.8186 and 0.7961, and Proposed-Med achieved 0.8548 and 0.8230. As shown in Table~\ref{tab:blink_type_recall}, relative to Proposed-Mean, Proposed-Med improved normal-blink recall by 0.0362 and long-blink recall by 0.0269. However, long blinks remained a mild residual weakness for the threshold pipeline, as recall decreased from normal to long blinks for three of the four conditions, with MNE-annot showing a slight increase for long blinks compared to normal blinks.
