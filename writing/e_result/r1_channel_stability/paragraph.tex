Within this frontopolar concentration, best-channel selection consistently favoured Fp1 and Fp2, although the precise choice between them varied across the four conditions (Table~\ref{tab:channel_selection}; Figure~\ref{fig:channel_selection}). In Raja, Fp2 accounted for 75/184 (0.408) selections and Fp1 for 67/184 (0.364), whereas the next most frequently selected channels were AF4 at 14/184 (0.076) and AF3 at 11/184 (0.060). In Cao2018, Fp1 accounted for 119/232 (0.513) selections and Fp2 for 57/232 (0.246), followed by F8 and Fz at 11/232 (0.047) each. However, full agreement on the exact best channel occurred in only 9/46 (0.196) Raja sessions and 6/58 (0.103) Cao2018 sessions, with mean pairwise agreement of 0.489 and 0.408, respectively (Table~\ref{tab:channel-robustness}). Pooled full agreement was 15/104 (0.144), and pooled mean pairwise agreement was 0.444. Thus, the selected scalp region was stable, but the precise choice between Fp1 and Fp2 was not.
