Beyond these condition-specific error profiles, Proposed-Med showed substantial variability across sessions and subjects, and the lowest-scoring sessions did not share a uniform failure pattern (Table~\ref{tab:best-session}). Across 104 sessions, session-level $F_1$ ranged from 0.9944 for \texttt{S21/S34\_20190122\_044130\_3} in Raja to 0.4167 for \texttt{S53/090925m} in Cao2018, with a median of 0.8730 and an interquartile range of [0.7659, 0.9360]. Across 44 subjects, mean $F_1$ ranged from 0.9928 for S55 to 0.5856 for S31, with a median subject mean of 0.8350. In Raja, 1 of 46 sessions had $F_1<0.6$ and 4 had $F_1<0.7$, whereas in Cao2018, 3 of 58 sessions had $F_1<0.6$ and 12 had $F_1<0.7$ (Table~\ref{tab:failure_analysis}). The lowest-scoring sessions were not predominantly associated with anomalously high ground-truth blink counts: the bottom-5 median was 0.9x the Raja dataset median and 0.3x the Cao2018 dataset median. The bottom groups were also not uniformly false-negative-dominated, with FN-heavy cases in 2/5 Raja sessions and 1/5 Cao2018 sessions.
