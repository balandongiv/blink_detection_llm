\midrule
\texttt{40\_exp1\_exp2\_\newline strategy\_\newline comparison.py}
&
\textbf{Exp 1 \& 2:} Compares all five conditions (BLINKER-concat, MNE-annot, DBO,
Proposed-Mean, Proposed-Med) on both Raja and murat2018 datasets at 60-second epochs.
Proposed-Mean runs before Proposed-Med (as specified in experimental design).
Includes per-session macro-F1 results, Wilcoxon signed-rank tests with Bonferroni
correction for 10 pairwise comparisons (one-tailed for proposed-vs-baseline,
two-tailed for Mean-vs-Med), and micro-/macro-averaged metrics per condition.
\\
\midrule
\texttt{41\_exp3\_epoch\_\newline duration.py}
&
\textbf{Exp 3:} Evaluates Proposed-Med (Strategy F with median estimator) across
four epoch durations: 20, 30, 60, and 120 seconds on both Raja and murat2018 datasets.
For each duration, re-runs the entire pipeline from scratch (Stage A--C) since
epoch-level statistics change with epoch length. Reports per-session macro-F1,
number of flagged epochs ($n_{\text{flagged}}$), and estimated threshold $\theta_c$.
Two-tailed Wilcoxon signed-rank tests compare each non-reference duration against
the 60-second baseline (Bonferroni correction for 3 comparisons).
\\
\midrule
\texttt{42\_exp4\_boundary\_\newline tolerance.py}
&
\textbf{Exp 4:} Tests Proposed-Med stability across five boundary tolerances
for event-level matching: 0, 50, 100, 150, and 200 milliseconds on both Raja and
murat2018 datasets. Runs the pipeline once per session and evaluates against all
tolerance levels to avoid redundant computation. Reports macro-averaged precision,
recall, and F1 at each tolerance, and computes the range of macro-F1 across the
five levels (range $<$ 1 percentage point indicates stability).
\\
\midrule
\texttt{43\_exp5\_user\_\newline rejection.py}
&
\textbf{Exp 5:} Simulates user-initiated pre-rejection of epochs by withholding
0\%, 20\%, and 40\% of valid epochs before Stage A. For each non-zero withholding
rate, 20 independent random draws are performed; session-level F1 is averaged
across the 20 replicates before Wilcoxon testing. Ground truth remains fixed
(blinks in withheld epochs count as false negatives). Two-tailed Wilcoxon tests
compare each non-zero rate against the 0\% reference (Bonferroni correction for
2 comparisons).
\\
\midrule
\texttt{44\_exp6\_\newline morphological.py}
&
\textbf{Exp 6:} Performs morphological analysis of detected and missed blinks on
both Raja and murat2018 datasets by extracting 500-millisecond waveform windows
centred on the peak of each blink event (both detected and ground-truth). Events
are categorized as true positive (TP), false negative (FN), or false positive (FP).
Waveforms are drawn from the frontal channel with the highest mean peak amplitude.
Generates butterfly plots (overlaid single-trial waveforms with mean highlighted)
for TP, FN, and FP categories per dataset, saved as PNG files for visual inspection
of failure modes.
\\
\midrule
\texttt{45\_exp7\_nmin\_\newline sensitivity.py}
&
\textbf{Exp 7:} Two-part analysis of the fallback threshold $n_{\min}$ (minimum
suspicious-epoch count for Stage B to use flagged epochs) on both Raja and murat2018
datasets.
\textit{Part A} (threshold-variance): For sessions with $\geq 20$ flagged epochs,
randomly subsamples the flagged set to $n \in \{2, 3, 5, 7, 10, 15, 20\}$;
for each $n$, computes Stage~B threshold over 50 independent subsamples and
records the inter-subsample standard deviation. The smallest $n$ at which variance
stabilises provides an empirical lower bound for $n_{\min}$.
\textit{Part B} (fallback-frequency): For each epoch duration in {20, 30, 60, 120} s
and each candidate $n_{\min} \in \{3, 5, 10\}$, reports the fraction of sessions
triggering fallback ($|\mathcal{B}| < n_{\min}$).
\\
