## Exact evidence (literal)

In this article, we demonstrate that by combining the features associated with electrocortical activities and eyeblink recognition analysis, it becomes feasible to design an accurate system for the inattention state prediction using just a single EEG sensor.

## Normalized evidence

The authors demonstrate that combining electrocortical activity features with eyeblink recognition analysis enables accurate inattention state prediction from a single EEG sensor.

## Evidence meaning

Ko et al. (2020) show that EEG recordings contain both neural activity (electrocortical) and blink information useful for fatigue/drowsiness prediction. This directly supports the manuscript's claim that EEG is valuable for both neural activity study and blink-related drowsiness markers.
