
This paper makes several contributions to blink detection in fatigue driving research. First, we propose  Candidate-Epoch-
Conditioned Thresholding (CECT), a three-stage pipeline that screens for candidate epochs likely to contain blink activity using multi-channel amplitude screening, derives a threshold for each channel from these candidate epochs, and applies each threshold to its corresponding channel across all epochs to extract channel-wise blink regions.
We evaluate a candidate-epoch-conditioned composition of CECT on an epoch-structured EEG recordings.
Second, we systematically compare the proposed pipeline against baseline methods, including the BLINKER-threshold baseline~\citep{kleifges2017blinker} and MNE~\citep{GramfortEtAl2013a}.
