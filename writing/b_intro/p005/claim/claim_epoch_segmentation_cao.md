## Exact evidence (literal)

Multi-channel EEG recordings during a sustained-attention driving task. [...] We present a 32-channel EEG dataset recorded from a sustained-attention driving task in a virtual reality environment.

## Normalized evidence

Cao et al. provide a multi-channel EEG dataset from a sustained-attention driving task, structured as a collection of recordings that are analyzed in epoch-based segments.

## Evidence meaning

Directly supports the claim that EEG recordings in fatigue-driving studies are segmented into epochs corresponding to task events. This dataset paper describes driving-task EEG recordings that are used by downstream analyses in epoch-structured form.
