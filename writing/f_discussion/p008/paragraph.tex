The present evaluation has several limitations that should temper interpretation of the results. First, the evaluation is limited to two driving-EEG corpora, Raja (closed) and Cao2018 (open) \citep{cao2019multichannel}, so generalisation to other paradigms, devices, and populations remains untested. Second, the method is single-channel and amplitude-threshold based; it does not exploit multi-channel spatial information or waveform morphology. Third, best-channel selection is only partly method-robust, with pooled mean cross-method agreement of 0.583 and full agreement among all four methods in 31.7% of sessions. Fourth, no comparison against deep-learning detectors is provided. Finally, the ground-truth blink annotations are dataset-specific, which may affect how directly the findings transfer to studies using different annotation protocols. Future work should validate the method on additional paradigms, devices, and populations, incorporate multi-channel and morphological information, improve long-blink recall, and benchmark performance against learning-based detectors.
