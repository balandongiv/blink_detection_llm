The spatial requirements of the pipeline were limited because a single frontopolar electrode recovered essentially all of the whole-cap oracle performance in both corpora. On Raja, Fp2 achieved a macro-$F_1$ of 0.868 compared with the oracle value of 0.8894, whereas on Cao2018, Fp1 achieved 0.797 compared with 0.8175. The optimum therefore localised consistently to Fp1/Fp2 despite differences in montage and acquisition hardware, while performance declined at electrodes farther from the eyes. This spatial consistency is compatible with the strong projection of blink activity to the prefrontal scalp and indicates that additional channels contributed little to blink-region detection once a frontopolar signal was available. The result also agrees with prior single-channel and forehead-EEG studies that detected blinks or derived fatigue-related measures from prefrontal recordings~\citep{chang2016detection,ko2020eyeblink,zhang2023method,tran2021detection,valderrama2018automatic,wang2025sliding,shahbakhti2022simultaneous,aoki2023drowsiness,lopezahumada2023hardware}. A dense montage is therefore not required for this task, which supports implementations based on a single dry or embedded forehead electrode in wearable and in-vehicle systems where setup time, comfort, and sensor count constrain deployment.
