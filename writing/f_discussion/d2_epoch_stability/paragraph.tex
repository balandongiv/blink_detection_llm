Experiment~2 showed that the proposed pipeline was largely insensitive to epoch duration at Fp1 and Fp2, the two strongest electrodes in Experiment~1 (Figure~\ref{fig:f1_by_epoch}). On Cao2018, neither electrode differed significantly from the 30-s reference at any tested duration. On Internal, durations from 10 to 40~s likewise produced no significant change, but longer epochs lowered $F_1$ significantly at Fp1 from 50~s onwards and at Fp2 only at 120~s. Even the significant reductions were small, the largest being 1.28 percentage points. The results therefore show no significant effect of duration from 10 to 40~s on either dataset or across the full 10--120-s range on Cao2018, although this absence of significance does not establish equivalence. This robustness is useful because epoch duration is usually set by the experimental paradigm rather than by the blink detector. Longer epochs may alter the composition of the candidate epochs used for threshold estimation, but the present analysis did not test this mechanism directly.
