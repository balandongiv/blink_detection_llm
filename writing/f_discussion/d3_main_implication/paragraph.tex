The margin of the proposed pipeline over the two continuous-signal baselines (Table~\ref{tab:f1_significance}) is a comparison of best-case configurations, because each method's channel was selected with the reference annotations. More informative than the margin itself is the way each baseline lost performance (Figure~\ref{fig:exp3_pr_scatter}). BLINKER~\citep{kleifges2017blinker} favoured recall over precision, which is consistent with a threshold lowered by pooling blink-heavy and blink-free epochs. MNE~\citep{GramfortEtAl2013a} showed no systematic bias but a wide spread of operating points, including sessions with very low recall, which is consistent with a range-based threshold raised by large excursions elsewhere in the recording. Neither mechanism was isolated, since BLINKER's threshold also differs from that of the proposed pipeline in its centre and multiplier. Both patterns nevertheless suggest that the signal pool from which a threshold is estimated affects where that threshold is placed. Such sensitivity is plausible because ocular-artefact amplitude varies across subjects, electrodes, and recording sessions~\citep{croft2000removal,guttmann2019new,klein2013reliable,kleifges2017blinker}.
