These findings are subject to several limitations. The benchmark was restricted to two driving corpora, so generalisation to other experimental paradigms or clinical populations was not tested. Event detection was reported under one overlap-based matching rule, and sensitivity to alternative event-matching criteria was not evaluated; absolute $F_1$ values should therefore not be compared across studies without accounting for their scoring rules~\citep{salles2024softed}. Performance was not reported separately by blink duration, so detection of prolonged eye closures, which is particularly relevant to drowsiness assessment, remains unestablished. Finally, the evaluation also depends on the available reference annotations and their operational definitions of blink onset and offset, boundaries that can vary with signal representation and annotation criteria~\citep{nystrm2024what}.
