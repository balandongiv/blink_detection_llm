The Raja to Cao2018 comparison showed different degrees of cross-dataset gap across methods, with performance higher on Raja than on Cao2018: the macro-$F_1$ gap (Raja minus Cao2018) was about $+0.069$ for Proposed-Med and $+0.142$ for MNE-annot \citep{cao2019multichannel}. This pattern indicated that the proposed method showed a small, consistent cross-dataset gap, supporting its consistency across the two evaluated datasets, whereas the MNE baseline degraded far more between corpora. Within the four-condition comparison, both proposed configurations significantly outperformed BLINKER-concat and MNE-annot (Wilcoxon, Bonferroni), with Proposed-Med obtaining the highest macro-$F_1$.
