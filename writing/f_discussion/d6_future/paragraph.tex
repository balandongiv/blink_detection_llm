There are several related topics that we would like to investigate in future studies, some of which follow directly from the limitations above. Reporting normal blinks and prolonged closures separately would establish whether performance is retained for the sustained closures most relevant to drowsiness assessment~\citep{tian2021fatigue,shahbakhti2023fusion}. Whether a single frontopolar channel can supply the spectral markers of drowsiness as well as blinks should also be examined, since it may carry theta and beta activity~\citep{ko2020eyeblink,shahbakhti2022simultaneous,aoki2023drowsiness} but has weaker alpha and strong ocular contamination. Reduced-channel systems built on these findings, and the pipeline itself, should be evaluated online under real driving conditions. Evaluation beyond driving data, including paradigms organised into task blocks, trials, or other natural segments, would test whether the observed robustness extends to different blink morphologies, cognitive demands, and recording conditions.
