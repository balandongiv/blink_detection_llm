Future work should therefore evaluate prolonged eye closures separately, test prospective channel selection, and broaden the benchmark. Reporting normal blinks and prolonged closures independently would establish whether performance is retained for the sustained closures most relevant to drowsiness assessment~\citep{tian2021fatigue,shahbakhti2023fusion}.
 Evaluation beyond driving data, including paradigms organised into task blocks, trials, or other natural segments, would test whether the observed robustness extends to different blink morphologies, cognitive demands, and recording conditions.
