The channel-choice analyses reported with Experiment~1 further showed that this spatial concentration did not translate into a stable choice of one electrode. Across the four conditions, Fp1 and Fp2 accounted for most selections: Internal selected Fp2 in 75/184 cases and Fp1 in 67/184, whereas Cao2018 selected Fp1 in 119/232 and Fp2 in 57/232. Nevertheless, all four conditions selected the same electrode in only 9/46 Internal sessions and 6/58 Cao2018 sessions, giving pooled full agreement in 15/104 sessions. The results therefore support stability at the level of the frontopolar region, but not interchangeability of Fp1 and Fp2 at the session level. Differences in ocular projection, electrode placement, and signal amplitude offer plausible explanations for this variability~\citep{plochl2012combining,guttmann2019new}, but these factors were not isolated in the present analysis. Moreover, because the reported best channel was selected retrospectively using reference $F_1$, the results do not demonstrate a deployable adaptive channel-selection rule; they instead motivate evaluating a label-free rule for choosing between frontopolar channels.
