Toward Principled Threshold Selection for Blink
Region Detection in Biological Signals

Author names omitted for review

 ABSTRACT This study evaluated an epoch-aware pipeline for EEG blink-region detection that avoided
  estimating detection thresholds from concatenated blink-containing and blink-free epochs. The three-stage
  pipeline first screened non-overlapping epochs by peak-to-peak amplitude using an autoreject-inspired cross-
  validation criterion, then estimated a sample-level threshold from the suspicious-epoch pool using either a
  mean-based or median/MAD-based estimator with a fallback for small pools, and finally extracted blink
  regions and mapped them to epoch-local coordinates. BLINKER-concat, MNE-annot, Proposed-Mean, and
  Proposed-approach were compared on the Internal and Cao2018 naturalistic driving-EEG corpora using
  event-level overlap matching and macro-averaged F1. At 30 s, Proposed-approach achieved a pooled macro-
  F1 of 84.03%, significantly exceeding BLINKER-concat at 72.56% and MNE-annot at 60.86%. Proposed-
  Mean and Proposed-approach were statistically indistinguishable (p = 1), which indicated that the gain
  came from epoch screening rather than the choice of Stage-B estimator. The channel-selection ablation
  further showed that a single frontopolar electrode was sufficient for the strongest single-channel detection
  on both corpora. Finally, at Fp1 and Fp2, the two electrodes identified by the channel-selection ablation,
  evaluated separately, pooled macro-F1 varied by only 0.41 and 0.50 percentage points, respectively, across
  epoch durations from 10 s to 120 s, and no tested duration differed from the 30 s reference after Bonferroni
  correction for either electrode.

3) Single-Channel Operation

The regional channel-subset analysis above established how
anatomical subsets of the montage behaved as self-contained
detectors; the present analysis takes this reduction one
step further and asks whether each individual electrode of
the full montage can independently support the complete
Proposed-approach detector. For every electrode retained
in the full-montage configuration, the complete Stage A­
Stage C pipeline was rerun using that one electrode alone,
with the same 30-second epochs, median centre, preprocess-
ing and thresholding parameters as the full-montage ref-
erence, so each result is a genuine self-contained single-
electrode detector rather than one electrode's score extracted
from a multichannel run.
